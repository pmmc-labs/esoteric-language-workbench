# Issues

Gaps in the language that the steps and the examples have found. Each is a
candidate for a proposal, and none is built. Under "Examples bend to the
language" (D6), a gap is recorded and the example changes, until the person
picks a proposal. Each entry says what was wanted, where it showed, and what is
open.

## Open

- **No operators.** `1 + 2` is not SMOL, so arithmetic is `1.plus(2)`. Left out
  of step 7 on purpose. Open: whether operators are wanted at all, and if so,
  whether they are method sugar or grammar. Not yet proposed.
- **No division.** There is no `divBy`, so there is no integer quotient or
  remainder. Left out of step 7 on purpose. Open: the rounding of a negative
  quotient, and what a zero divisor does. Not yet proposed.
